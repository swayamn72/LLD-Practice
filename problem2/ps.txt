Problem Statement: Smart Parking Lot Billing Engine
Background
You are building the backend architecture for an automated parking lot system. 
The lot handles different types of vehicles, and management wants a 
flexible billing system because pricing rules change frequently.

Core Requirements
Design a system that satisfies the following conditions:

Vehicle Baseline: The system must support at least two types of vehicles: 
Car and Bike. Every vehicle must have a license plate string.

Ticketing: When a vehicle enters, the system generates a Ticket. 
The ticket must store the vehicle information and the entryHour (an integer).

Billing Calculation: When a vehicle exits, the system requires the 
ticket and the exitHour. It calculates the fee based on the vehicle type:

Bikes: $20 flat fee for the first hour, + $10 for every additional hour.

Cars: $50 flat fee for the first hour, + $30 for every additional hour.

Note: If a vehicle stays for 1 hour or less, they only pay the flat fee.

Lot Manager: A central class (e.g., ParkingGate) that handles vehicles entering and exiting.

Design Constraints

Extensibility (OCP): Management plans to add ElectricCar next month, 
which gets a 20% discount on the final bill. Your design must allow adding 
new vehicle types and pricing logic without heavily modifying the ParkingGate 
or Ticket classes.

Polymorphism: The Ticket shouldn't need to know the exact math for every 
single vehicle type. It should rely on the vehicle to know its own rate.

Memory Management: Ensure your solution handles memory safely using smart pointers.

Example Execution Flow

[Entry] Bike 'MH-01-AB-1234' entered at hour 10. Ticket issued.
[Entry] Car 'MH-04-XX-9999' entered at hour 11. Ticket issued.

[Exit] Bike 'MH-01-AB-1234' exiting at hour 13.
-> Stay duration: 3 hours. 
-> Total Bill: $40 (20 + 10 + 10).

[Exit] Car 'MH-04-XX-9999' exiting at hour 12.
-> Stay duration: 1 hour. 
-> Total Bill: $50 (50).
